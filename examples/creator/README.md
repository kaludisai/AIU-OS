# Fictional creator exercise

## Brief

Draft a 60-second beginner explainer about preparing a simple weekly meal plan. Audience: someone who has never meal-planned. Format: spoken script of roughly 120-150 words, followed by three shot ideas. Tone: friendly and practical.

## Supplied source notes

A meal plan lists the meals you intend to prepare. Check the food already available, choose a few meals, and make a shopping list for missing ingredients. A plan can reduce last-minute decisions. No measured savings, nutrition advice or exact prices are provided.

## Request

`/plan-one-task Use examples/creator/README.md as the complete fictional brief. Draft the spoken script and shot ideas in personal/practice/creator-output.md. Mark it proposed and cite these notes. Do not post anything.`

## Acceptance checks

- Defines meal planning in plain language.
- Gives a first action a beginner can take today.
- Contains roughly 120-150 spoken words and three shot ideas.
- Makes no unsupported savings, health or price claims.
- Keeps shot directions separate from spoken copy.
