# setup-workspace

1. Ask one question at a time: what kind of work they do, one current goal, the first repeatable task they want help with, working/output preferences, and what actions need their review.

2. Allow skipping.

3. Never request credentials or private account exports.

4. Save only confirmed answers to personal/brief.md; keep unanswered details visibly unknown.

5. Personal source files belong under personal/.

6. Link relevant source paths instead of pasting everything into the startup manual.

7. Show the saved brief and one next task.

8. Preserve an existing brief and its user edits; ask before replacing confirmed content.

9. Keep the fictional practice files intact.

10. Do not connect accounts, schedule jobs or publish anything.
