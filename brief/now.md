# Fictional practice brief

This is example data for learning, not the distributor's personal information.

Goal: draft a simple birthday-dinner plan for six adults, on Saturday at 6 p.m. Exact calendar date is not supplied.

Constraints: $120 total food budget; one vegetarian guest; oven, stovetop and refrigerator available; familiar food preferred. Prices must be labeled estimates until actually checked. Include a complete vegetarian meal option.

Deliverable: proposed menu, estimated shopping list, preparation order, important unknowns and one next action. No menu is approved. No purchases or bookings are authorized.
