# Keep the template public and your work private

This repository contains fictional inputs and independently authored starter files. It does not contain the creator's private operating system.

Personalization goes into `personal/`, ignored by Git. Practice outputs and local settings are also ignored. Gitignore is a convenience, not a security boundary or a retroactive removal tool. Review staged files before any push. A fork starts public unless you deliberately choose otherwise; cloning a public repository does not publish your local edits by itself.

Never add credentials, personal account exports, customer records, private health/financial data or raw conversation histories to a public copy. Keep different clients in deliberately scoped workspaces. Record sources and decisions in the smallest relevant place.

Before sharing: inspect the exact files, verify rights to any third-party material, check the repository's visibility and confirm no personalized files are staged. Do not publish a copy of the folder you use for your whole life or business.

No accounts, telemetry, scheduled jobs or external access are installed by this kit. Use your agent application's actual permission controls in addition to these instructions.
